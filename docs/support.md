# Support

Blue Luchador LLC  
Decoy — Live Break Assistant

If you need assistance with Decoy, please contact us at:

**[support@blueluchador.com](mailto:support@blueluchador.com)**

## What to Include in Your Message

To help us respond quickly, please include:

- The email associated with your trial or subscription
- A description of the issue
- The date and time the issue occurred
- Screenshots if applicable

## Billing Questions

For billing, subscription, or refund inquiries, please include:

- The date of the charge
- The last four digits of the card used (if available)
- A brief description of the issue

## Response Time

We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.

## Service Issues

If you believe there is a technical issue affecting capture, checklists, or chat, please include:

- The platform being used (e.g., livestream marketplace)
- A description of what occurred
- Any error messages shown

Thank you for using Decoy — Live Break Assistant.
